Privacy

This is the personal academic website of Laure Berti-Equille. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It exists to share research, publications, tutorials, and contact information. It is not a commercial service, does not sell anything, and does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal information.

What the site collects

The site itself does not collect or store personal data from visitors, and there are no contact forms. Some pages under /website/ include Google Analytics, which records aggregate, anonymised usage statistics (such as page views and approximate region) to help understand which content is useful. This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. No advertising or cross-site tracking is used by this site.

Hosting and logs

As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard technical information such as IP addresses and browser user-agents for security and operational purposes. That processing is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

External links

This site links to external profiles and resources — for example ORCID, Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, ResearchGate, GitHub, LinkedIn, and Medium. Once you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that external service applies.
